What is the SLO Wine Mixer

A wine festival for people who like to have fun.

The SLO Wine Mixer is the Central Coast's high-energy wine festival, built for a new generation of wine lovers. One ticket, unlimited tastings from local wineries, and a room full of good people who would rather laugh than sniff and spit.

We trade the velvet rope for a mechanical bull, a live band, and a DJ. We trade the lecture for "No Snob" wine classes that actually make tasting fun. Think coastal cowboy energy, big pours, and the best Saturday San Luis Obispo has seen.

Year 1 sold out and packed the room. Year 2 lands Saturday, April 3, 2027, and it is going to be bigger. Get on the list so you do not miss the drop.

The Experience

More than a tasting

Six reasons the SLO Wine Mixer hits different. Show up thirsty, leave with a new favorite winery and a story.

Unlimited Tastings

Pour after pour from dozens of Central Coast wineries. One wristband, no limits, just bring your glass back.

Live Music & DJ

A live band and a DJ keep the energy up from the first pour to the last call. This is a party, not a museum.

Mechanical Bull

Coastal cowboy energy, literally. Grab your hat, hold on tight, and let the crowd cheer you on.

"No Snob" Wine Classes

Quick, fun, judgment-free classes that teach you what you actually like. No swirling rules, no shame.

Photo Activations

Neon signs, props, and backdrops built for the feed. Round up your crew and make the memory permanent.

The Wine Window

A throwback Tuscan tradition, reborn Central Coast style. Walk up, get poured, keep the night moving.
